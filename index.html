Our story

A room worth lingering in.

Step off King Faisal Road into a quiet space of dark upholstered chairs, warm cream walls and golden ring lights. P3 is made for slow conversations, unhurried afternoons and a good cup in your hands.

The same gold-on-black language carries onto the cup, with “The essence of Kashmir” as P3’s signature line.
